# Privacy is structural, not a runtime filter

Because Smith Wiki and Bluesky are public and "you can't prompt privacy into a
research agent" (an established claim on the wiki itself), we do not rely on the agent
to filter private content at runtime. Instead privacy is structural: the human
approves the *question* (by reposting it into `#research`), Research output is Clean
and Atomic with no anchor back to the Seed thread, and the output is web-grounded
synthesis rather than a republication of the private discussion. The Seed thread's
content is never carried into a public artifact.
